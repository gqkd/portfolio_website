About me

background & motivations

I'm an AI & Data Engineer with roots in Biomedical Engineering, a background that taught me to work across very different domains, from healthcare to banking to industrial systems. I hold a 110/110 Master's from Politecnico di Torino with an AI and Data orientation, where I focused on machine learning, signal processing, and the data systems behind them.

Today I work at Qvantia, where I lead the design of a proprietary AI and data platform, shaping its architecture from the ground up. Before that, I built scalable ETL pipelines and data validation frameworks for the banking sector, and spent years working across the full data stack: from requirements and data modeling to pipeline development, cloud migrations to Azure, and BI delivery for clients across a range of industries.

The way I work starts with the whole system, not the single component. Before writing code, I think about the architecture that has to hold everything together, how the pieces connect, where they'll strain under load, and what keeps them reliable as they scale. It's the difference between shipping something that runs and building something that lasts.
